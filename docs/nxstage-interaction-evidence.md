- The [Manitoba Renal Program’s System One setup procedure](https://www.kidneyhealth.ca/wp/wp-content/uploads/50.20.08.pdf) also describes raising the handle until it clicks and pulling the door open while keeping the handle up. This supports the order of interaction for System One specifically.

Its figures 2 and 4–6 warrant comparison with a variant-matched open-door reference before authoring.

Author separate movable nodes with stable IDs, then verify motion and occlusion from several camera angles. Do not infer a dial merely from a circular shape: verify its function and travel against the device variant and source evidence.
