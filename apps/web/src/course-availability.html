Course availability

Organization administrators control access to each published release. Public courses appear in the catalog. Unlisted courses can be found through their link. Private courses do not accept new candidate enrollments.

Changing visibility can replace the course link. Existing enrollments retain their progress. Independent enrollment does not create organization membership.

Availability and learner disclosures

Candidate enrollment is free. Credentials represent organization-issued product training, not professional licensure or regulatory approval.
